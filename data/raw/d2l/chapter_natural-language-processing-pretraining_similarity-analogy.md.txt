Word Analogy.

Besides finding similar words, we can also apply word vectors to word analogy tasks. For example, “man”:“woman”::“son”:“daughter” is the form of a word analogy: “man” is to “woman” as “son” is to “daughter”. Specifically, the word analogy completion task can be defined as: for a word analogy <math>, given the first three words <math>, <math> and <math>, find <math>. Denote the vector of word <math> by <math>. To complete the analogy, we will find the word whose vector is most similar to the result of <math>.
