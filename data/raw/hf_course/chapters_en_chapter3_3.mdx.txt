💡 Key Takeaways:

The <code> API provides a high-level interface that handles most training complexity.

Use <code> to specify your tokenizer for proper data handling.

<code> controls all aspects of training: learning rate, batch size, evaluation strategy, and optimizations.

<code> enables custom evaluation metrics beyond just training loss.

Modern features like mixed precision (<code>) and gradient accumulation can significantly improve training efficiency.
