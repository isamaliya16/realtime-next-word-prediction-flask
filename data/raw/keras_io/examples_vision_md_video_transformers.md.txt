Building the Transformer-based model.

We will be building on top of the code shared in this book chapter of Deep Learning with Python (Second ed.) by François Chollet.

First, self-attention layers that form the basic blocks of a Transformer are order-agnostic. Since videos are ordered sequences of frames, we need our Transformer model to take into account order information. We do this via positional encoding. We simply embed the positions of the frames present inside videos with an <code> layer. We then add these positional embeddings to the precomputed CNN feature maps.
