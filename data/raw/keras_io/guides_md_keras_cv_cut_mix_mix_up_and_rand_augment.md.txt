Overview.

CutMix and MixUp: generate high-quality inter-class examples.

<code> and <code> allow us to produce inter-class examples. <code> randomly cuts out portions of one image and places them over another, and <code> interpolates the pixel values between two images. Both of these prevent the model from overfitting the training distribution and improve the likelihood that the model can generalize to out of distribution examples. Additionally, <code> prevents your model from over-relying on any particular feature to perform its classifications. You can read more about these techniques in their respective papers:
