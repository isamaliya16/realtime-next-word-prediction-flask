Explicit horizontal fusion with foreach_map and torch.compile.

Horizontal fusion is a key optimization in ML compilers. In eager, this is typically expressed using the torch.foreach ops which parallelizes operations across a list of tensors. However, supporting all possible permutations of arguments is quite difficult (e.g. mixtures of scalars and lists). Foreachmap allows conversion of any pointwise op in <code> to a horiztonally fused foreach variant. In this tutorial, we will demonstrate how to implement the Adam optimizer with <code> to generate a fully fused kernel.
