Warmstarting model using parameters from a different model in PyTorch Partially loading a model or loading a partial model are common scenarios when transfer learning or training a new complex model. Leveraging trained parameters, even if only a few are usable, will help to warmstart the training process and hopefully help your model converge much faster than training from scratch.
