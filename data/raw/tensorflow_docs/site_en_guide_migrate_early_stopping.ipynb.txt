This notebook demonstrates how you can set up model training with early stopping, first, in TensorFlow 1 with <code> and an early stopping hook, and then, in TensorFlow 2 with Keras APIs or a custom training loop. Early stopping is a regularization technique that stops training if, for example, the validation loss reaches a certain threshold.

In TensorFlow 2, there are three ways to implement early stopping:

Use a built-in Keras callback—<code>—and pass it to <code>.

Define a custom callback and pass it to Keras <code>.

Write a custom early stopping rule in a custom training loop (with <code>).
