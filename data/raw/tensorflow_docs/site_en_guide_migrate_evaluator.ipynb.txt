Evaluation is a critical part of measuring and benchmarking models.

This guide demonstrates how to migrate evaluator tasks from TensorFlow 1 to TensorFlow 2. In Tensorflow 1 this functionality is implemented by <code>, when the API is running distributedly. In Tensorflow 2, you can use the built-in <code>, or a custom evaluation loop on the evaluator task.

There are simple serial evaluation options in both TensorFlow 1 (<code>) and TensorFlow 2 (<code> or <code>). The evaluator task is preferable when you would like your workers not switching between training and evaluation, and built-in evaluation in <code> is preferable when you would like your evaluation to be distributed.
