In TensorFlow 1, you use <code> to monitor and log tensors, while <code> helps stop training at a specified step when training with <code>. This notebook demonstrates how to migrate from these APIs to their equivalents in TensorFlow 2 using custom Keras callbacks (<code>) with <code>.

Keras callbacks are objects that are called at different points during training/evaluation/prediction in the built-in Keras <code>/<code>/<code> APIs. You can learn more about callbacks in the <code> API docs, as well as the Writing your own callbacks and Training and evaluation with the built-in methods (the Using callbacks section) guides. For migrating from <code> in TensorFlow 1 to Keras callbacks in TensorFlow 2, check out the Migrate training with assisted logic guide.
