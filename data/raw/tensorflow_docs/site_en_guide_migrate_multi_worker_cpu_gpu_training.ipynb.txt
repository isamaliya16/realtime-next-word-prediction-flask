This guide demonstrates how to migrate your multi-worker distributed training workflow from TensorFlow 1 to TensorFlow 2.

To perform multi-worker training with CPUs/GPUs:

In TensorFlow 1, you traditionally use the <code> and <code> APIs.

In TensorFlow 2, use the Keras APIs for writing the model, the loss function, the optimizer, and metrics. Then, distribute the training with Keras <code> API or a custom training loop (with <code>) across multiple workers with <code> or <code>. For more details, refer to the following tutorials:
