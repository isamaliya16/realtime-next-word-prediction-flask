In TensorFlow 1, to customize the behavior of training, you use <code> with <code>. This guide demonstrates how to migrate from <code> to TensorFlow 2's custom callbacks with the <code> API, which works with Keras <code> for training (as well as <code> and <code>). You will learn how to do this by implementing a <code> and a <code> task that measures examples per second during training.

Examples of callbacks are checkpoint saving (<code>) and TensorBoard summary writing. Keras callbacks are objects that are called at different points during training/evaluation/prediction in the built-in Keras <code>/<code>/<code> APIs. You can learn more about callbacks in the <code> API docs, as well as the Writing your own callbacks and Training and evaluation with the built-in methods (the Using callbacks section) guides.
