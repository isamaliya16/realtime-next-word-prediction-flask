How to read this. Validation accuracy is the share of held-out positions where the model's single top word equals the word that actually came next; top-5 counts the true word anywhere in its five best guesses. These are metrics for a next-word classification task on documents the models never saw in training. They are not a guarantee of fluent or factually correct language, and many different words can be reasonable continuations. Training accuracy is measured with dropout active, so it is not directly comparable to validation accuracy. All three models use the same data split, vocabulary, hyper-parameters and evaluation code.
